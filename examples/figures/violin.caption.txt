Synthetic activation Gibbs-energy errors (prediction minus reference), n = 32 reactions per illustrative predictor. Points show all observations; horizontal offsets separate coincident values. Solid half violins show Gaussian kernel density estimates with bandwidth h = 0.35 times the sample SD; dashed curves use 2h. Each density is scaled to the same maximum width.
