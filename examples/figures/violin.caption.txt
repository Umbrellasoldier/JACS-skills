Small synthetic samples for comparing observations and density estimates. Population: 32 synthetic reactions per group. Observation unit: reaction. Quantity definition: Signed activation Gibbs-energy error = prediction minus reference. Plotted observations: Baseline, n = 32; Transfer, n = 32; Refined, n = 32. Half violins: Gaussian KDE, equal maximum width; segments: medians. All observations are shown beside the densities. Bandwidth factor: 0.35; shape is bandwidth-sensitive. Point offsets affect only the categorical coordinate. The number of KDE modes changes when the bandwidth factor is doubled for: Baseline, Transfer. Density lobes alone do not establish subpopulations.
