# Field sources used for vocabulary calibration

Checked 2026-10-26. These public papers are a calibration set for terminology and rhetorical
functions in computational reaction chemistry. They are not a frequency study and do not define
an official JACS house style. The skill uses paraphrased observations rather than copied prose.

Peer-reviewed journal articles are the default evidence for the vocabulary in this reference.
Published conference papers may supplement them when their publication status is clear. Preprints,
manuscripts, and early versions are discovery-only sources and must be labeled explicitly; they do
not establish a preferred expression when a published source is available.

## How to use the sources

- Use a source to check the meaning and collocation of a term, not to copy a sentence.
- Verify publication status and the DOI before calling a work `published`; label conference
  papers, preprints, and early versions separately. Prefer the published source when the same
  terminology is available in both versions.
- When the user asks for the latest literature, search current publisher or primary-paper pages,
  record the search date, and state the scope of the search. Do not claim an exhaustive review
  from a short search.
- The user's earlier HERMES drafts and project-specific terminology supplied the local learning
  signal for this skill. Their raw text, figures, results, and paths are not included here.
