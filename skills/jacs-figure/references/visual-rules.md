# Visual guidance and its evidence

Read the [figure corpus](../../jacs-shared/references/figure-corpus.jsonl),
[visual annotations](../../jacs-shared/references/figure-annotations.jsonl) and
[conditional rule cards](../../jacs-shared/references/figure-rules.jsonl) when source calibration
is useful. The pilot visually reviews selected figures, not every panel of every paper.
Physical fonts and line weights were not measured from these raster sources.

The Article observations support these conditional choices:

1. Assign complementary evidence roles to multi-panel figures: question, comparison,
   chemical explanation, validation or boundary. There is no fixed panel count or hero panel.
2. Keep the same entity's colors consistent across structures, plots and process diagrams.
   Exact colors remain configurable; the sample includes vivid, pastel and monochrome designs.
3. Align comparable plots and structural views when their quantities and purposes match.
   Different quantities can require different ranges; equal parity limits are a design choice.
4. Place state names, energy values and structural identifiers near the objects they explain.
   Reference states, units and conditions still need an explicit caption or specification.
5. Combine colors with meaningful markers, line styles, hatching or direct labels.
6. Use chemically identified structures or cases to explain aggregate predictions when they
   contribute evidence, rather than adding an unrelated molecule as decoration.

These map to JF01–JF06 in the rule cards. Communication examples are kept as a separate
stratum and do not inflate Article support. Source counts are by paper as well as by figure;
authors and topics overlap, so they are not independent draws from the whole journal.

Counterexamples matter: A07 uses saturated colors and bordered workflows; B06 includes a
serif/full-frame comparison; F01's enthalpy parity plot has unequal ranges; F05 uses prominent
motif highlights and circled panel letters. Do not declare a unique JACS palette, universal
sans-serif typography, mandatory open axes or lowercase-only panel labels from this sample.

The supplied style sheet and palette are **project defaults**, chosen for usable first
renders. The official size/type limits live only in
[graphics policy](../../jacs-shared/references/graphics-policy.md). Preserve the user's existing
semantic colors and scientific notation unless changing them is part of the request.

Column-width and label-reading guidance also reflects the pilot forward-test feedback. It
is an implementation preference and was not added to the frozen corpus observations.
