# Editing decisions

## Chinese notes to English prose

Resolve the subject and logical relationship before translating. Chinese notes often omit
whether a number is an absolute result, a difference, a relative change, or a conditional
success fraction. Retain an explicit gap if the notes do not resolve it. Translate technical
terms consistently; do not use synonyms to vary a defined chemical quantity.

Separate what was measured or computed from what the authors infer. Select tense according
to whether the sentence describes a performed action, standing property, or interpretation.
Active and passive voice are both useful; no measured universal ratio is supplied here.

## Compression

Remove repeated claims, generic significance statements, and methodological detail that does
not help interpret the current result. Keep the comparison, magnitude, applicable conditions,
and limits. When a caveat affects a headline claim, shortening it must not remove its effect.
Rework a long sentence into two when the evidence and inference are otherwise hard to separate.

## Strength of wording

Match verbs to what the evidence establishes. A trend without an inferential analysis should
not become statistical significance. The word `significantly` is not categorically banned;
check the intended claim and context. A small or absent difference should not become
`comparable` without a justified comparison. Likewise, an indirect mechanistic interpretation
does not become proof because `demonstrates` reads more confidently.

## Synthetic example

Input facts: candidate geometries were optimized; frequency analysis found one imaginary
mode in the accepted structures; no IRC results were supplied.

Supported rewrite: “The accepted optimized structures each exhibited one imaginary frequency.”
If relevant to the requested passage, add: “Reaction-path connectivity remains to be verified.”
Do not append a statement that the structures connect the intended endpoints.

This example is original and synthetic. It is not quoted from a JACS article or a real project.
