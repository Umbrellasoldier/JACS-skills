# JACS policy snapshot

Target: *Journal of the American Chemical Society*, CODEN `jacsat`.
Source: [official author guidelines](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=jacsat).
Page updated: 2026-08-27. Checked: 2026-09-17. Recheck exact requirements for formal submission.
Use the snapshot date when an answer depends on an unrefreshed JACS requirement; a routing
answer need not add unrelated policy metadata. JACS Au is a separate journal.

| Applicability | Requirement or guidance |
|---|---|
| Article and Communication abstracts | The guidance is about 250 words maximum; retain the qualification “about.” |
| Communication | No article-style section headings. The 2200-word limit includes abstract, main text, and graphic titles, footnotes, and captions; references and manuscript title/authors/affiliations are excluded. A four-page template is not the defining limit. |
| Article | Label appropriate sections; experimental details may be in SI. A conclusion is recommended. |
| Title | Avoid the prohibited words “First” and “Novel.” Abbreviations need broad familiarity across chemistry. |
| Initial Fast Format | References may use any complete style, including article titles; do not confuse this with production formatting. |
| Computational manuscripts | The method must suit the problem and provide significant chemical insight or substantial predictive value. |
| Article and Communication | An abstract and TOC graphic belong in the manuscript; SI is a separate submission file. |

Apply only relevant checks. A language edit does not require a submission-package intake.
Automatic word counts are estimates; confirm the final selection boundaries in the submission
document. Editorial norms, approximate quantities, and corpus preferences must not become
invented hard limits. The page linked above remains the source for requirements outside this snapshot.
