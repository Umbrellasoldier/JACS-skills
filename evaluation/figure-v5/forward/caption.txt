Figure 1. Comparison of Control and Candidate predictions of activation Gibbs energy (ΔG‡) for 12 paired synthetic reactions. (a) Signed errors, defined as prediction minus the supplied reference value. Blue circles and apricot squares denote Control and Candidate, respectively; gray connectors link the same reaction, and the dashed vertical line marks zero error. All 24 observations are shown without exclusion or coordinate offsets. (b) Descriptive summaries for the same 12 reactions: mean signed error, mean absolute error (MAE), and root mean squared error (RMSE). These are, respectively, the mean of the signed errors, the mean of their absolute values, and the square root of their mean squared values; all quantities are in kcal mol−1. Candidate has lower MAE and RMSE. Control has a mean signed error closer to zero because its −12 kcal mol−1 error for Q001 offsets +1 kcal mol−1 errors for the other 11 reactions. No uncertainty intervals or hypothesis tests are presented. The reference method, temperature, solvent, and standard state were not specified. All values are synthetic teaching data and do not establish research performance.
