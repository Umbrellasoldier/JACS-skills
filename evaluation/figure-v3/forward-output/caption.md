**Figure 1. Synthetic demonstration of method-evaluation displays.** All data are synthetic and do not constitute research results. (a) Supplied signed errors for the same 36 reaction IDs evaluated by Direct, Adapted, and Calibrated. All 108 observations are plotted; light gray lines connect the same reaction across methods. Horizontal category jitter is deterministic and shared within each reaction; error values are unchanged. Boxes span the first to third quartiles, center lines mark medians, and whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges of the box. Quartiles use linear interpolation, and all observations outside the whiskers are retained. The dashed horizontal line marks zero error. The error-subtraction order was not supplied. (b) Supplied mean absolute error (MAE) values from eight independent synthetic runs per method at each training size. Small open symbols show all runs; larger filled symbols and error bars show the arithmetic mean ± sample standard deviation, calculated with denominator 8 − 1. These bars describe run-to-run spread and are not confidence intervals. Training counts are displayed on a base-2 logarithmic axis. Connecting mean values provides visual guides, with no fitted scaling law. (c) Supplied MAEs for neutral and charged categories on a common 0–3 kcal mol⁻¹ color scale. The hatched cell labeled “Not evaluated” preserves the missing Calibrated/Charged entry; no value was imputed. Across panels, method names and categorical encodings are consistent. Target quantity, research conditions, evaluation splits, and evaluated population sizes for panels b and c were not supplied; the three source files are not assumed to share an evaluation population. No inferential tests were performed.
